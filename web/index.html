Keys follow the physical US layout, so on a German keyboard the bottom row starts at Y. Gold arc = value stored in the song. Red arc and dot = what the module plays right now with its PERFORM macros (Main = decay, X = colour, Y + CV in 2 = filter). Macros are offsets and are never saved.
